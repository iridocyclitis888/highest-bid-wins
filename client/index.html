HOW TO PLAY

  1. Everyone joins the same room with the 4-letter code (3 to 10 players).
  2. Each round, one player is the Auctioneer. They pick a topic and secretly enter the items (3 for each bidder). They don't bid that round.
  3. Everyone else gets a budget: $20 with 3 players, plus $10 for each extra player. It resets every round.
  4. Items come up one at a time in random order. You never know what's coming next, so spend wisely!
  5. The first bidder opens at $1 automatically. On your turn, tap +$1, +$3 or +$5 as many times as you like, then press the red BID button to lock it in. Or PASS. You have 30 seconds.
  6. If someone outbids you, your money comes back. Passing only takes you out of that item. The last bidder left pays their bid and wins the item.
  7. Out of money? You sit out. If nobody has any money left, the rest of the items are handed out for free.
  8. After each round, everyone votes for the best haul. The winner gets 1 point.
  9. When everyone has been Auctioneer, the most points wins. There are silly consolation awards too!
